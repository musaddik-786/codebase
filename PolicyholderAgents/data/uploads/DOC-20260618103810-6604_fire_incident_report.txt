FIRE INCIDENT REPORT

Report ID: FIR-2026-073
Date of Incident: March 14, 2026
Time of Incident: 02:35 AM
Location: 24, Green Valley Street, Maple Residency

1. INCIDENT SUMMARY:
A residential fire broke out in the kitchen area of a single-family house during the early hours of March 14, 2026. The fire was reportedly caused by an unattended gas stove left on overnight.

2. RESPONSE DETAILS:
Local fire services were alerted at approximately 02:42 AM by a neighbor who observed smoke coming from the property. Fire engines arrived on site within 8 minutes.

3. DAMAGE ASSESSMENT:
- Kitchen area sustained severe fire damage
- Living room experienced moderate smoke damage
- Electrical wiring in the affected region was compromised
- Estimated property damage: INR 4,50,000

4. CASUALTIES:
- No fatalities reported
- One resident suffered minor smoke inhalation and was treated on-site

5. FIRE CAUSE:
Preliminary investigation indicates the fire originated due to a gas stove left unattended with a utensil overheating, eventually igniting nearby materials.

6. ACTION TAKEN:
- Fire contained and extinguished within 25 minutes
- Gas supply shut off
- Ventilation provided to disperse smoke

7. RECOMMENDATIONS:
- Install smoke detectors in all rooms
- Ensure gas appliances are turned off before sleeping
- Conduct regular electrical maintenance checks
